Website Classifier

This is a machine learning model that classifies website as benign, malicious or AI generated based on the DOM of the website.

The model is not always correct and the more extreme a website is, the more likely it is to be misclassified. The berkshire hathway or ismycomputeron.net are good examples of websites that are misclassified as malicious - dont ask why - i am yet to do the ablation studies.

Equally simple websites with little content are misclassified as AI generated See: motherfuckingwebsite.com (and of course the better version bettermotherfuckingwebsite.com ) This is more a note to myself to check when i have tuned the model more.
